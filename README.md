# 📊 Financial Analytics Project — Top 500 Companies in India

## 🗂️ Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard delivering a comprehensive view of the financial performance of India's top 500 companies. Designed for investors, analysts, and researchers, this dashboard enables dynamic exploration of market capitalization, sales performance, and company-level insights — all in one place.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏦 **Market Capitalization** — Visual breakdown of market value across India's top companies, offering a high-level understanding of their standing in the market.
- 📦 **Sales Quantity Analysis** — Charts and tables revealing each company's sales performance and relative market share.
- 🏢 **Company Information** — A detailed, searchable list of all 500 companies along with their key financial metrics.

---

## ⚙️ Capabilities

- 🔍 **Interactive Filters** — Focus on specific companies or industries for a deeper, targeted market analysis.
- 🔎 **Drill-Down Support** — Explore individual company financials in granular detail beyond the summary view.
- 🎨 **Customizable Layout** — Adaptable visualizations and dashboard layout to fit varied reporting and presentation needs.

---

## 🎯 Goals

- ✅ Deliver a clear and concise overview of the financial performance of India's top 500 companies.
- ✅ Identify key market trends, patterns, and performance indicators.
- ✅ Enable faster, evidence-based investment and business decisions.
- ✅ Serve as a shared reference point for stakeholders exploring the Indian market landscape.
